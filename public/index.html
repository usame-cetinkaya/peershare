    Install PeerShare
    Opens like an app, one tap from your home screen.
    1. 1Tap the Share button in Safari
    2. 2Choose “Add to Home Screen”
    3. 3Tap “Add”

    Activity

    Your screen stays on until this finishes. Keep PeerShare open: switching apps or locking the phone pauses it.

      Files and messages go straight from device to device and are encrypted on the way. Nothing is stored.
